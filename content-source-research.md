Continue only with official public source pages/files or material with clear permission and preserve URL, edition/version, page reference, and validation status for every record.

## 2026-08-18 — Official HSC curriculum evidence

The official NCTB page titled **“এসএসসি ও এইচএসসি পরীক্ষার পুনর্বিন্যাসকৃত পাঠ্যসূচি…”** is available at <https://nctb.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dff5933eb65569e24d31>. The page identifies itself as last updated on 23 June 2025 and provides official-source download links for HSC restructured syllabi for 2021–2025, including a 2025 HSC and equivalent syllabus link. This page is suitable as a curriculum/pattern evidence record, but it is **not** by itself a license to reproduce question-paper text or a verified answer key.

The first content release will therefore use this NCTB page as curriculum evidence only, then require a separately captured official textbook or official board-paper source and an independently verified answer/explanation record for every question before publication.

Its public HSC list includes a 1 February 2026 item titled **“এইচএসসি ও সমমান পরীক্ষার প্রশ্নপত্র সেটার এবং মডারেটরদের প্রশিক্ষণ ম্যানুয়াল”** and multiple public notices relating to HSC question-setting and moderation training in January 2026. It does not provide a publishable official HSC ICT question bank or answer key, so it will be recorded as a question-quality/process source rather than copied into student practice.

## Pilot decision

The first pilot will be a deliberately small **HSC ICT fundamentals** set, beginning with number-system concepts and basic web/programming concepts that can be expressed as original questions with deterministic answers. The project database currently has no HSC ICT subject or chapter taxonomy, so the pilot will first add the minimum ICT subject, textbook, and chapter records. Each question stem and explanation will be newly authored; the NCTB page will be retained only as curriculum-alignment evidence. No private, leaked, or third-party-scraped exam item will be imported.

## Expanded source-registry policy

The registry will cover Bangla- and English-version requirements separately wherever the underlying curriculum, edition, or authorized source differs. The initial discovery list is: Bangla, English, ICT, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Higher Mathematics, Accounting, Finance/Banking/Insurance, Business Organization and Management, Production Management and Marketing, Economics, Geography, Civics, History, Sociology, Logic, Islamic Studies, and relevant admission-preparation patterns. This is a discovery scope rather than a claim that every subject has the same official book, examination pattern, or online availability.

For each candidate source, the registry must record the publisher or issuing organization, source URL, language/version, edition or year, curriculum/subject relationship, access date, permitted-use basis, and whether it can support: **curriculum alignment only**, **original-question development**, or **question-text reuse with documented permission**. The system will not download, mirror, upload, or expose a book file merely because a PDF or Word document appears online. It will link students to the authorized original where appropriate and publish only independently reviewed MCQs with answer-key and explanation evidence.

### Unverified catalog lead

`https://www.nctb.cloud/textbooks/2026` presents itself as an NCTB textbook catalog and links to the older `nctb.gov.bd` site, but the alternate domain has not yet been confirmed by a government-domain statement. It is therefore classified as a **discovery lead only** and must not be stored as an authorized book source, used to download files, or cited for question publication unless a verifiable official-domain confirmation is found.

## Published pilot — HSC ICT fundamentals

An eight-question original HSC ICT fundamentals pilot has been created for the 2025–26 taxonomy across **Number System and Digital Device** and **Web Design and Programming Fundamentals**. It is source-linked to the active NCTB curriculum evidence version, and every record was checked for four options, exactly one correct option, a linked evidence record, and an explanation. The questions deliberately test deterministic fundamentals such as binary conversion, hexadecimal conversion, nibbles, HTML links, and basic C operators. They are original MCQ GURU questions, not copied board-paper or textbook questions.

## Registry expansion — taxonomy and language separation

The 2025–26 registry now includes core common, science, humanities, and business-studies subject entries, including first and second papers where applicable. Source, textbook, and question records now preserve a **language version** so Bangla and English material cannot be silently conflated. Each source also carries an access classification. The registry is intentionally broader than the current published question bank: a subject is not student-available until an active source version, mapped chapter, and complete approved original question batch have been verified.

The [Dhaka Education Board HSC Corner](https://dhakaeducationboard.gov.bd/index.php/site/product/hsccorner) is registered only as an official public reference for question-setting and moderation governance. It is not treated as a question bank and no file from the page has been ingested.
