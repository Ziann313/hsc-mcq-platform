# All-Groups and Boards Brief Compatibility Record

## Scope

This record reconciles `MCQ_GURU_Prompt_3_Complete_All_Groups_Boards.docx` with the live MCQ GURU platform. The requested coverage includes protected previous-year board/admission papers, official patterns, cut-offs, eligibility rules, and large content volumes. Those materials cannot be copied, recreated, or described as historical without documented reuse authority and review evidence.

## Implemented group-safe preparation flow

The HSC preparation page now presents all three groups before subjects. Selecting a group resets the subject/chapter filter, limits live published-capacity discovery to that academic group, and persists the group in the exam preset. When a student starts, the server applies the group filter while selecting and freezing questions. A student cannot turn a Humanities or Business Studies setup into mixed or Science content simply by changing client-side controls.

## Validation

Database-backed regression coverage verifies that each of the three groups has its own Bangla capacity response and that selected questions all resolve to the requested academic group. The full regression suite, production build, and mobile HSC-preparation direct route were checked after the change. No source-reuse status, question answer key, or official-pattern availability was weakened.
