**Prepared by:** Manus AI  
**Scope:** MCQ GURU’s metadata-only ingestion of the supplied Bangladesh HSC master curriculum and book catalogue.

## Summary

The supplied catalogue has been normalized into the MCQ GURU registry as **60 paper-level rows**, and every row now maps to a platform subject. This establishes complete **taxonomy coverage**, not complete question-bank coverage. Student-facing practice remains deliberately limited to questions that are original, source-linked, independently answer-checked, explained, approved, and published.

> A commercial listing, paid course, or unverified website can prove that a title or course exists. It cannot authorize copying textbook pages, questions, answer keys, PDFs, or lesson materials into MCQ GURU.

## Source-Use Decision Register

| Source category | Records | Permitted role in MCQ GURU | Not permitted |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Official curriculum and governance evidence | 3 | Curriculum alignment, metadata confirmation, and moderation guidance | Copying full books or creating questions without independent review |
| Supplementary instructional references | 2 | Chapter discovery only | Reuse of lessons, assessments, solution text, or PDFs |
| Commercial catalogue/course leads | 2 | Title and availability discovery only | Downloading, rehosting, copying questions, keys, PDFs, or course materials |
| Blocked unverified lead | 1 | None until independently authenticated | Any content or metadata claim beyond the recorded lead |

The official NCTB higher-secondary listing is the primary lawful evidence for the available listed titles and versions. The Dhaka Education Board HSC Corner is retained as quality-governance evidence rather than a question bank. [1] [2]

## Implementation Boundary and Next Content Sequence

The catalogue gives MCQ GURU a complete structure for all listed HSC subjects, including optional subjects by stream. It does **not** grant the right to collect or redistribute commercial books. New question release should proceed in this order: first attach an eligible official or licensed curriculum-evidence source to a mapped chapter; then draft original questions; then validate four options, one correct answer, explanation, and source linkage; finally submit to protected review and publish.

The appropriate next expansion candidates are the second papers for the already verified science subjects, then Bangla and English common papers that are explicitly present on the official NCTB listing. Humanities and business-studies content must wait for source-level verification of curriculum scope or a clear license; commercial retail and coaching links remain discovery-only.
